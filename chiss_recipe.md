# Csapla Stew

## Ingredients
- 2 kg blue-tinted snow crabs from Csilla's arctic waters
- 500g frozen winter root vegetables
- 4 Csilla frost peppers, dried and crushed
- 3 tablespoons greel oil (substitute: olive oil)
- 1 cup fermented marine algae (substitute: seaweed)
- 2 liters glacial water (substitute: filtered water)
- 5 tablespoons pulverized kivu spice
- 1 handful of luminescent traq mushrooms
- 6 cloves black rhizal

## Instructions

1. Heat greel oil in a cauldron over a low-power fusion heater.
2. Crush the rhizal with the back of a ch'itleq blade and add to oil.
3. Add frost peppers and allow to infuse for 7 minutes.
4. Break crab shells using traditional Chiss ice hammers and add to cauldron.
5. Pour in glacial water and bring to precisely 92°C.
6. Add root vegetables and reduce heat to 87°C for 34 minutes.
7. Introduce kivu spice gradually while stirring counterclockwise with a bone ladle.
8. Add fermented algae and traq mushrooms during the final 9 minutes.
9. Serve in heated obsidian bowls with a side of frozen ch'etf bread.

A proper serving honors the Fifth Family by arranging the crab pieces in a formation resembling the Starcrest constellation as viewed from Csilla's northern hemisphere during winter solstice.
